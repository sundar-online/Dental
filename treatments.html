Wisdom Tooth Gentle Extraction

Impacted, tilted, or partially erupted third molars frequently trap bacteria, damage adjacent molars, and cause severe facial swelling. As a Maxillofacial Pathologist and Chief Dental Surgeon, Dr. Barath Balaji performs atraumatic, painless wisdom tooth extractions with rapid socket recovery.

Procedure Time 20 – 30 Minutes
Initial Recovery 2 – 3 Days
Surgical Technique Micro-Surgical Piezo Sectioning
Comfort Protocol Zero Pain Intra-Operatively

Maxillofacial Surgical Impaction Care

Minimally Invasive Sectioning • Nerve Protection Protocol

Pain-Free Protocol

Step-by-Step Surgical Workflow

Removing an impacted wisdom tooth is done with extreme surgical finesse to safeguard the inferior alveolar nerve:

1

Digital Radiography

High-res panoramic x-ray maps root anatomy, angulation, and distance from mandibular canal.

2

Profound Numbing

Targeted local anesthetic blocks ensure total numbness of the jaw, teeth, and cheek tissues.

3

Gentle Sectioning

Tooth is atraumatically divided into smaller segments for effortless, force-free removal.

4

Hemostatic Dressing

Socket is flushed with sterile saline and dressed with sterile gauze to encourage immediate clotting.

When is Extraction Necessary?

✓ Recurrent Pericoronitis: Painful, swollen gum flaps over partially erupted molars that trap food.
✓ Preventing Adjacent Decay: Impacted teeth lean against second molars, causing cavities between roots.
✓ Relieving Jaw Stiffness: Eliminates radiating ear aches, difficulty opening mouth, and cheek biting.
✓ Cyst & Pathology Prevention: Prevents dentigerous cysts from forming around unerupted crowns.

Critical Extraction Aftercare

Do not spit forcefully or drink through straws for 48 hours to prevent dry socket.
